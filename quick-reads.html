50 very short, legal online reads

These are free public-domain eBooks or illustrated book editions. Most should take about 5–30 minutes, though poetry/nursery books can be quicker and a few longer picks can run closer to 40 minutes.

Important for a school reading log: these are whole short eBooks, but many are children’s picture books, poems, or fairy tales. Only count them if your teacher accepts very short books. Don’t pretend a short story is a full-length novel.
