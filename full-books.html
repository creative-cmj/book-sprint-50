50 short, full nonfiction books

Space, war/history, countries, and science. These are real books—not articles—chosen from English full-text results in Open Library. Most matching editions are about 30–64 pages, which is roughly 30–45 minutes for many readers.

How to read: Click a book, make a free Open Library account, then use Read or Borrow. Availability can change because some books are digital library loans. The page count is for one short edition, so a different edition may have a different number of pages.
