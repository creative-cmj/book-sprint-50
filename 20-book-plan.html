You were right: the old list had a few choices that were too young. These replacements use middle-school topics and stronger vocabulary, but are short, image-heavy nonfiction editions so they can be finished in around 30–60 minutes by many 7th graders.

Honest heads-up: A publisher’s exact grade level can vary by edition, and reading speed is different for everyone.
